# Thesis editing instructions

Before editing prose anywhere under this directory, read
`ACADEMIC_WRITING_GUIDE.md` in full and apply it as the default writing and
review standard.

Direct user, supervisor, programme, and examination requirements take
precedence. Do not substitute the conference-specific rules in
`paper/STYLE.md` for the thesis guide. Verify empirical numbers against their
authoritative result artefacts, and preserve the guide's distinctions between
generation, validation, execution, downstream use, simulation, and hardware
evidence.

## Mandatory user approval for thesis content

Before adding, replacing, or deleting any content intended to appear in the
thesis, present the exact proposed text in the conversation and wait for the
user's explicit approval of that version. This gate covers, at minimum,
chapter prose, abstracts, headings, captions, table text, footnotes, and
appendices. Discussion of an idea, approval of an outline, or approval of an
earlier wording is not approval of a new or revised passage.

Until that approval is given, do not write the proposed content to a thesis
source file, stage it, or commit it. After approval, write only the approved
text. Any substantive wording change requires a new in-conversation preview
and approval before it is written or committed. Read-only inspection,
analysis, planning, and compilation do not require approval provided that they
do not alter thesis content.
